How it works

Welcome to Illinois Engineering Jobs.

This board is for civil engineering students, recent graduates, and working engineers who want to see what is open across Illinois in one place. Every listing here is a real opening at a firm doing IDOT or Illinois Tollway work, collected from that firm's own careers page and kept current by hand. No accounts, no applying through a middleman, and nothing to pay. You find the role here, and you apply on the firm's own site.

I track the open roles at the 195 firms working on IDOT and Illinois Tollway projects, and I collect each one from the firm's own careers board, so you do not have to. Of the 800 roles here, 559 link straight to the exact posting (one via a labeled Indeed fallback, the rest on firm sites); the remaining 241 are listed as leads until I can verify a direct link. Pay is posted for 321 roles; the rest are honestly marked "No pay posted" rather than left blank.

Getting started

How to use this board

Four steps take you from landing here to an application on a firm's own site.

1

Search or filter

Use the search box at the top for a title, firm, city, or keyword, or open the Jobs tab and filter. Every filter accepts several picks at once, so you can choose a few cities, firms, fields, pay bands, or company sizes together. Pick Chicagoland under Area to cover Chicago and its suburbs in one tap. A job shows when it matches any pick inside one filter and every filter you have used.

2

Read the labels

Each card carries plain labels for experience level, license, pay, and link status. The glossary right below shows each label exactly as it appears on a card, so there are no surprises when you start browsing.

3

Apply on the firm's own site

Tap Apply and you go straight to that job's exact posting on the firm's own site. That is where you apply. Nothing is applied for, uploaded, or collected on this page.

4

Save jobs for later

Tap the bookmark on any card to add it to your Saved list, then tap Saved at the top to see only those roles. Your list stays on your own device, no account is needed, and clearing your browser data clears it.

Small print, plainly

Good to know

Listings change between checks

I recheck listings on a rolling cycle, and each card shows when I last checked it. A role can still close, or a firm can move a posting, between my check and your click. If an Apply link lands somewhere unexpected, the WhatsApp note below is the fastest fix.

Pay is shown as annual

To keep postings comparable, I show pay as an annual amount. Hourly postings are converted at 2,080 hours a year and marked with "≈", and the firm's own wording always stays on the card underneath.

I do not work for these firms

I compile this board on my own from public postings. I cannot influence hiring, pass along a resume, or answer for a firm's process. Please apply through the firm's own posting.

Share the link, not copies

I keep the underlying list myself and refresh this page from it. Sharing the link to this page means people always see the current version; a copied list goes stale within days.

Spot a broken link or a wrong listing?

Tell me and I will fix it for the next person. Every job card also has a small "Link broken? Tell me" link that starts the message with the job and firm already filled in.
